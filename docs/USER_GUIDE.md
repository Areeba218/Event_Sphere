# EventSphere user guide

## Everyone
1. **Create an account** (Register): choose your role, accept the Privacy Policy, then open the verification link emailed to you.
2. **Sign in.** Forgot your password? Use *Forgot password* on the sign-in page.
3. The 🔔 bell shows notifications (approvals, schedule changes, session reminders, messages). Updates also appear live without reloading.
4. **Profile** lets you edit details, change your password, choose whether your email/phone are shared, download your data or delete your account.

## Attendee
* **Events** – browse, search, *Register*, *Bookmark*, *Contact organizer*, view *Details*.
* **Schedule** – choose an event; *Register* for or *Bookmark* sessions. **My schedule** lists them; you are reminded ~30 minutes before each starts.
* **Exhibitors** – search by keyword, category and event; *View on floor plan*; *Message* an exhibitor.
* **Floor plan** – click a booth to see the exhibitor, products and to message them.
* **Feedback** – send suggestions or report issues with an optional rating.

## Exhibitor
1. **My applications** → fill the form (company, category, products, logo, documents) and submit.
2. Wait for approval (you are notified). You can still **Edit profile** (logo, description, contacts).
3. **Floor plan** → pick the event, click a free booth → *Reserve*. The organizer confirms. You can cancel a *reserved* booth; confirmed booths are released by the organizer.
4. Click your booth to enter products, details and staff and to see **neighbouring exhibitors** (message them or use their contact details if shared).

## Organizer
1. **Events** → create an expo (draft or published) with theme, dates, capacity. Edit or delete anytime (registered attendees are notified of changes).
2. **Exhibitors** → review applications, add a note, *Approve* / *Reject*. Rejecting frees any booth held.
3. **Floor plan** → *Generate booths* (count + booths per row); click a booth to assign an approved exhibitor, confirm a reservation or release it.
4. **Schedule** → add sessions (speaker, topic, room, times). Room clashes are blocked. Editing/deleting notifies registered and bookmarked attendees.
5. **Analytics** → live registrations, exhibitors, booths, engagement, booth traffic, session popularity; *Export CSV report*; system health.
6. **Feedback** → mark items *in review* / *resolved* (the author is notified).
